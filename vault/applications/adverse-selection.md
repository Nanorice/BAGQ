# Adverse selection

**Interview form:** You got filled. Is that good news or bad news? What is your edge if you are picked off X% of the time?

**Built from:** [[conditional-information]]
**Pays into:** [[market-making]]

## Notes

The quote that gets hit is the one that was wrong. Modelling the conditional distribution of the
price given you were filled, and pricing that cost into the spread.
